## Short description (132 char max)

## Detailed description
Memlace turns every AI conversation into memory you own.

Save chats from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Copilot, and AI Studio with one click — right from the composer. Search everything with ⌘K. Continue any conversation in a different AI with one keystroke.

Everything is stored locally in your browser. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

**What you get**
• Save conversations with a native button in each AI's composer
• Universal search across memories, chats, and projects
• Projects, tags, pins, and favorites to organize your thinking
• "Continue in another AI" — copies an optimized context and opens the new chat
• Knowledge engine that indexes languages, frameworks, and tools you discuss
• Optional AI Center — bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, or LM Studio key for summaries, semantic search, and Ask Memlace (RAG)
• One-click full backup and restore
• Beautiful side panel, popup, and dark-mode-aware UI

**Privacy**
Memlace has no servers. Your data stays in your browser. If you enable AI features, requests go directly to the provider you configured — never to us.

**Keyboard shortcuts**
• Alt+Shift+P — open Memlace
• Alt+Shift+S — save selection
• ⌘K / Ctrl+K — universal search
